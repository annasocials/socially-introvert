Questions

Before you ask.

I can get a big content package for cheaper. Why this?

Those packages are wonderful if what you need is volume. This is for you if what you need is direction. A small set of connected pieces, each with one clear job: moving the right person from your feed to your website. Quality of path, over quantity of posts.

How fast will I see results?

Social traffic can show up within days of posting. Blog search traffic typically compounds over 2–4 months, which is exactly why it’s an asset, not an expense. Your 30-day report shows the leading indicators (impressions, clicks, saves) early.

Do I need to be on calls?

No. Fully async: one shared Notion, one approval checkpoint per week, voice-note friendly.

Perfect, we build on it. The research phase maps what’s already working and the new posts slot into your existing structure with internal links.
