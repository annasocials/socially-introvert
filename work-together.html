The process

Here’s how working together goes, from choosing a service to the final handoff.

Browse the services and pick the one that fits where your brand is right now. Each service page explains what’s included, what’s not included, the investment and the expected timeline.

You don’t need a discovery call to decide. If you’re not sure which one fits, send me a few lines about your business and I’ll tell you where I’d start.

See the services

Once you’ve chosen, submit your booking and complete your payment. You’ll receive a confirmation and your onboarding instructions by email.

I’ll send a project questionnaire, plus instructions for sharing your brand assets, website links, references and any account access the project needs. One form means you never repeat yourself across messages.

Once your payment, questionnaire and materials are in, I begin. You’ll have a shared project workspace where deliverables, progress, references and feedback stay organised in one place.

I work asynchronously. You’ll get updates in your project workspace, with recorded video walkthroughs and voice notes whenever something needs explaining.

Live calls aren’t part of one-time services. Monthly management includes a monthly call.

When the agreed work is complete, you receive your final deliverables, files, links and a recorded walkthrough. I make sure you know what was done, where everything is stored and what to do next.

For one-time projects, this is where our project concludes. For monthly management, we continue month to month under the agreed scope.

Payment options

I want the investment and the payment process to be clear before we begin.

Up to $500

Full payment upfront to confirm your booking.

One-time projects above $500

Pay in two instalments: 50% to confirm your booking and begin, and 50% before the final deliverables and handoff.

Monthly services

Payable monthly in advance, on the agreed billing schedule. The minimum commitment for the service still applies.

Your timeline starts once I’ve received your initial payment, completed questionnaire and any required materials or access. Work outside the agreed scope is discussed and quoted separately.
