FAQ

Questions before you book

I’m confident in my work, so I keep everything clear upfront: what’s included, what isn’t, what it costs and how payment works.

Which service should I choose?

Choose Strategy if you need a clear marketing direction, Setup if you know what you need but want the tools and workflows configured for you, and Ongoing support if you want someone to help you do the work each month. Not sure? Book an intro chat and I’ll point you to where I’d start.

How do I get started?

Send an inquiry. You don’t need a perfect brief, just tell me what’s going on with your brand. If you’d rather talk first, book an intro chat, a conversation about your business and what feels stuck, with no pitch. Then we pick where to start, I research and build, and you publish and keep going.

Who are these services for?

Growing F&B and modern lifestyle brands that are creating content and want it to work harder: a clearer plan, tidier systems, or a steady partner for the day-to-day marketing.

How long does each project take?

Simplify Content Ecosystem Strategy takes 14-20 days. Simplify Coded Pinterest, Customer Journey Planning, Content Management Systems, AI-Assisted Content Workflows, Pinterest Account Setup and Blog Marketing are each one-time projects of about 2 weeks.

Are there minimum commitments?

The one-time projects are single projects with no ongoing commitment. All three ongoing support services (Simplify Pinterest Management, Simplify Integrated Funnel Pinterest and Simplify Studio) have a minimum 4-month commitment, because that’s how long it takes to build real momentum.

How does payment work?

Every project is paid upfront, and that’s how we lock in your start. Anything over $500, including the monthly services, can be paid in two instalments, so you can spread the cost without delaying the work.

Do you offer refunds?

There are no refunds once payment is made. I’m confident in my work, and I make everything clear before you pay: each service lists exactly what’s included, what isn’t, and how we measure value. Not sure yet? Book an intro chat first and we’ll talk it through, with no pitch.

How quickly can we start?

Fast. I onboard you within 48 hours of payment, so you’re not left waiting to get moving.

What’s the difference between Pinterest Management, Integrated Funnel Pinterest and Simplify Studio?

Pinterest Management ($700/month) is consistent Pinterest execution. Integrated Funnel Pinterest ($1,000/month) is Pinterest execution plus wider marketing funnel strategy. Simplify Studio ($850/month) is a flexible strategic and implementation partnership across agreed marketing projects, up to 10 hours a month.

I want to manage Pinterest myself. What’s the best fit?

Simplify Coded Pinterest ($350 founding price) gives you the strategy to run it yourself: keyword research, board architecture, a six-month content calendar, around 48 tailored Pin concepts and a DIY implementation guide. If you’d like the account built for you instead, choose Simplify Pinterest Account Setup.

Do you write the blog posts, design the Pins or run my social media?

It depends on the service. Pinterest Account Setup includes design and copy for 10 starter Pins and scheduling of approved Pins, and Pinterest Management includes 90 designed and scheduled Pins a month. Blog Marketing gives you the strategy, calendar and briefs but doesn’t include writing or publishing the articles, and Simplify Studio doesn’t include daily social media management or unlimited content creation. Each service lists exactly what’s not included.

How will I know it’s working?

Every service has agreed measures of value, such as outbound clicks, qualified website visits, enquiries and email sign-ups, time saved on content tasks, or account readiness. The monthly Pinterest services include performance reporting and a check-in.
