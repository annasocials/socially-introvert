Chapter 7

Algorithmic Feed
Training.

How to manually force Pinterest's AI to understand your exact niche before you post.

The 72-Hour Warm-Up

0 Posts + 10 Niche Saves + 100% Pruning

You must map your account to established algorithmic nodes before distributing content.

Step-by-Step Execution

  • 1. Consume Only (72 Hrs): Do not post original content. Search only for your target keywords. Act like your ideal customer.

Chapter 13

Visual Story
& Layouts.

My eye doesn't know where to look first. We must fix visual clutter.

The Lifestyle Composition Formula

1 Hero Product + 3-5 Props + Negative Space

Pick ONE story. Make the hero item large. Leave negative space for text overlays. Use odd numbers of items (3, 5) as it is psychologically more pleasing.

Step-by-Step Layout Fixes

  • 1. Mixed Intent: Do not combine self-love quotes, skincare, and home decor in one collage. Pick ONE clear theme.
  • 2. Focal Point: Use the Rule of Thirds. Place your hero item on an intersecting grid line. Reduce the scale of supporting items.
  • 3. The Background Scene: Add a subtle "scene feel" behind cutout products (a blurred faint vanity) instead of a harsh solid color.

The Ultimate Visual Test

Ask: "Would someone want to LIVE in this image?" If no, redesign it. Pinterest is an aspiration engine.

Chapter 16

5-Angle
Image Match.

YESSS this is one of the BIGGEST reasons pins either flop or fly 😭📌

The Master Formula

Hook Title + Visual Proof + Clear Focal Product + Text Overlay

Title = Promise. Image = Proof. If they mismatch = low saves + low clicks.

Step-by-Step Execution: Map your 5 Angles

1. Inspiration Title (Reach)

Ex: "Cozy Bedroom Ideas for Fall Nights"

Best Image: Lifestyle room image. Top 70% beautiful room, Bottom 30% text overlay. Warm lamp glow, soft tones.
Why: People save the feeling. They're collecting ideas. Top funnel.

2. Product Title (Clicks)

Ex: "This Lamp Made My Room Feel Cozy"

Best Image: Close-up product in lifestyle setting. 70% product, 30% surrounding vibe. NOT plain white BG.
Why: Click-driven. They instantly see what caused the transformation.

3. List Title (Saves)

Ex: "5 Things Every Aesthetic Bedroom Needs"

Best Image: Collage 2x2 or 3x1 grid. Each image = one item. Use bold numbers ("5 MUST-HAVES ✨").
Why: People love scannable lists. High saves. Great for affiliate.

4. Problem / Solution Title

Ex: "Your Room Feels Boring? Try This"

Best Image: Before/after split. Left: boring room. Right: cozy upgraded version. Text: "Before → After".
Why: Instant transformation content. Pinterest LOVES this.

5. Seasonal Title

Ex: "Summer Bedroom Refresh Ideas"

Best Image: Visual must match season mood. Spring: pastel. Summer: airy whites. Fall: warm lighting. Winter: deep textures.

Simple Image Match Cheat Sheet

Title Style Best Image
cozy room ideas full room scene
amazon finds collage
this lamp changed my room close-up lifestyle
5 things every room needs list collage
room boring? before-after

Big Mistakes to Avoid

  • — PRODUCT ONLY ON WHITE BG: Looks like ad creative. Low saves. Always lifestyle context.
  • — TITLE MISMATCH: Title says "room ideas" but image only shows one close-up lamp. Instant bounce.

Chapter 28

Capsule Blog
Architecture.

A capsule blog is the smartest way to build a blog that grows fast. Keep content organized around ONE cluster.

The Capsule Formula

1 Pillar Post (1800 words) + 5 Cluster Posts (Linked Back)

Step-by-Step Execution

  • 1. Pillar Post: This is your ultimate guide. Example: "Ultimate Coquette Bedroom Decor Guide". Cover everything: bedding, lighting, storage.
  • 2. Cluster Posts: Create 5 smaller posts supporting the main theme. (e.g., "10 Coquette Bedding Ideas", "Pink Room Decor Under $50").
  • 3. Internal Linking (SEO Web): Every cluster post MUST link back to the Pillar post. The Pillar post MUST link out to all cluster posts. This makes Google understand your niche fast.

What to Ignore

Do not write isolated, random posts (e.g., one about budgeting, one about dog food). Build one Topic Capsule at a time. Once one capsule is done, repeat for another niche.
