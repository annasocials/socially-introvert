What happens week by week

Plus a short check-in email every week, so you always know what's happening.

Week 1

Map

I review your existing content, website, and visibility opportunities.

  • What's already working
  • What content has more potential than it's getting credit for
  • Where people could actually discover your business
  • What we'll focus on during the project

You get a focused Simplify Map — a personalized roadmap showing exactly how to turn your existing content and account into better reach: what to fix, what to repurpose, and what to publish first.

Week 2

Repurpose + Optimize

You don't need more content — you need your existing content to go further. I select up to 5 pieces (blog posts, educational content, social posts, product content, thread posts, stories, carousels, reels, etc.) and turn them into new strategic opportunities.

Alongside that, I optimize the Pinterest foundation: profile, board structure, naming + descriptions, keyword direction. Plus targeted SEO/content recommendations for key pages — not a redesign, just what makes existing content easier to find.

Week 3

Implement

This is the part that makes Simplify 30 different — I'm not handing you a to-do list. I create up to 8 Pinterest pins from the opportunities we identified and implement the agreed visibility work myself, within the project.

Week 4

Measure

We set a baseline before implementation, then track what was discovered, what got engagement, what generated clicks, and which content/topics showed the most potential. You get a simple end-of-project review with what I'd recommend next.
