Why I'm your bestie.

I don't do calls.

I know. Most marketers say "let's hop on a discovery call." I won't. However you communicate with your actual best friend that's how we work.

01

We work async, always

You don't need to block out an hour, do your hair, or perform "professionalism" for me. Send me a voice note while you're packing orders. Reply to my email at 11pm when you finally have a second to breathe. I'll be there, and I'll make sure everything stays seamless.

02

I've actually done the thing

Before I built Socially Introverts, I was deep in the pinterest and blog world. I know what it's like to stare at a blank content calendar and feel like you're shouting into the void, and I know what it's like to have 200 product photos and zero idea which ones to post.

03

I know when it clicks

When one blog post starts bringing in search traffic. When one Pinterest pin keeps getting saves and sales years later. When your content finally starts working while you sleep. That's the feeling I build for you.

04

Trust runs both ways

I'm not going to micromanage you. Please don't micromanage me. I do my best work when you trust me to handle the discovery side so you can handle the making side.

05

I actually like the quiet work

Keyword research at 10pm? Love it. Building a Pinterest board structure while listening to a podcast? Perfect. Repurposing your blog post into 5 pins without bothering you once? That's my ideal Tuesday.

06

I'm also a 9-5 worker. I get it.

I'm not sitting around waiting to hop on a Zoom. I'm building your content system between my own meetings, during my lunch break, at 10pm when the house is quiet — just like you're making your products in the stolen hours of your own day. I respect your time because I barely have enough of my own.

07

Your product is a lifestyle, not an ad

Most product brands post product photos and wonder why nobody engages. Here's the truth: people don't buy products, they buy the life your product fits into. I find those lifestyle moments — the slow morning with your mug, the confidence of wearing your earrings, the cozy home with your candle — and build your entire content ecosystem around them.

Pinterest for Search → Blog for SEO → Instagram for Lifestyle + Discovery

You make things. Really good things. No calls. No daily posting. No "constantly show up." Just your work, discovered by the right people, in the lifestyle context it deserves.
