How many Pins you get

The number is agreed with you and confirmed before onboarding. Each month starts with a short list of what your business needs people to find. I call each item a focus. Every focus gets about three Pin variations, so we can see which angle Pinterest picks up.

A focus can be:

  • A product or collection you want to sell more of
  • A seasonal moment coming up, like gifts or holidays
  • A blog post, guide or offer that needs traffic
  • A past winner worth refreshing with a new Pin

Small candle shop

about 9 Pins

3 focuses: two hero scents and one gifting moment. Fewer products, so each one gets deeper testing.

Jewelry brand

about 18 Pins

6 focuses: three collections, a Mother’s Day push, one blog guide and one old winner refreshed.

Digital product launch

about 12 Pins

4 focuses: the new product, a free preview, the launch offer and a how-to guide. Pins are planned ahead so they are live when you launch.

Larger catalog

about 22 Pins

Too many focuses for one month, so the Pin Map picks the top 7 and moves the rest to later months.

These examples show how the Pin Map works. Your own map sets your real number. If it needs more Pins than the month allows, we move a focus to next month or add a Pin Boost. Nothing gets cut silently.

One loop each month

1

Map the opportunity

I review your priority products or services, seasonal opportunities, existing content, search intent and website destinations. You approve a tailored Pin Map before any design begins.

2

Create with purpose

I design an agreed number of original Pins from your brand assets. Each Pin gets search-focused copy and a clear destination, with creative angles that fit the content and the customer journey.

3

Publish strategically

I schedule approved Pins to relevant boards, check destination links and plan around your priorities and seasonal timing. Publishing starts once the strategy and required assets are ready.

4

Learn from the data

I review available impressions, saves, outbound clicks and top-performing content, then use what I find to guide future creative decisions. Not every Pin performs equally, and that is useful to know.

5

Simplify the next month

You get a concise monthly report and a Loom walkthrough. The next Pin Map builds on what we learned, so the strategy evolves instead of starting from scratch.
