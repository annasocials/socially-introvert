How the first month works

It starts with understanding your business, moves into the work, and then uses what we learn to set the next month.

Onboarding · before publishing

Phase 1: Understand the business

I review your website, social media, products or services, existing content, brand positioning, customer journey and available Pinterest analytics.

I also ask about your priorities, important offers, target customers, seasonal campaigns and what success looks like for you.

You get: a clear picture of your business, its current discovery path and the opportunities worth exploring.

Month 1 · planning

Phase 2: Map the strategy

I use the research to choose priority products or services, search themes, content angles and website destinations. You review a Pin Map before I create any content.

You get: an agreed monthly plan that connects your offers to Pins and URLs.

Month 1 · execution

Phase 3: Make and publish

I create the agreed Pins, write search-focused titles and descriptions, schedule the content and check the destination links. You review the creative batch at the agreed approval point. Once you approve it, I handle publishing.

You get: the agreed content, prepared and published on schedule.

End of month 1

Phase 4: Review and refine

I review the available data, look at early content signals and write a short report.

You get: a review of what was published, what we can see so far and what deserves more testing.

After month 1

Phase 5: Strategy call and next steps

We meet to go over the first findings, your priorities, what we learned and the direction for next month. It is a focused strategy session, not a weekly meeting. Afterward I write down the agreed priorities and continue asynchronously.

How the ongoing months work

From month two, the same loop repeats: review the signals, plan the next batch, create, publish and report.

The strategy call after month one sets the direction. You decide whether to have future calls or keep the reviews async.
