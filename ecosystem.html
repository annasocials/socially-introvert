How performance is reviewed

Each month you receive a performance overview based on the data available from the platforms we publish on, such as impressions, saves, clicks and top-performing content. I use it to guide what we create next. Not every piece performs equally, and that’s useful to know.

I can’t promise specific traffic, rankings, enquiries or sales, and I’d be careful with anyone who does. I build everything with strategy and search in mind. Results depend on your products, your website, demand and the consistency of the work over time.
