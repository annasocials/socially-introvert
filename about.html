What I Do

You make the things. I market them.

I help small, creative, visual, product-based businesses turn what they already have into useful, connected marketing. Instead of treating every piece of content as a one-time post, I look at the bigger picture:

  • What could people discover through search?
  • What questions could become content?
  • What existing content could be repurposed?
  • Where else could this idea be distributed?
  • What needs to be planned, organized and kept moving?

The goal is to build a content system that supports your products without requiring you to constantly start from scratch.

You make the things. I market them.

I'm happiest behind the scenes — researching, organizing, connecting ideas, repurposing content, working with search opportunities, editing short-form content and making sure the marketing actually gets done.

I don't want to make you create more just for the sake of creating more. I want to make the things you've already made work harder.
