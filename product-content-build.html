  • What should eventually lead them back to the product?

How a Product Content Build comes together 🍋

01

I get inside the business

I'll look at your:

  • products
  • website
  • existing content
  • audience
  • customer questions + reviews
  • current marketing
  • business priorities

I'm looking for the things that could become marketing conversations. That might be:

  • a product story
  • a customer problem
  • a lifestyle moment
  • an opinion
  • a recurring question
  • a search opportunity
  • a campaign idea
  • a seasonal angle
  • a content series

You don't need to arrive knowing what any of that is. That's my job.

02

I find the bigger world around your product

Your product doesn't have to carry your entire content strategy. I'll look at the world around it:

Product → Person → Lifestyle → Problems → Desires → Stories → Conversations

This gives us somewhere to go beyond "here's our product." And it gives your content more ways to attract people who may not be actively looking to buy yet.

03

I decide where the ideas should live

You don't need to be everywhere. I'll recommend the channels that make the most sense for this particular build.

Instagram

visual storytelling, product education, brand connection and campaigns

Threads

founder opinions, stories, ideas, conversations and personality

Pinterest

search, discovery and evergreen product-related content

The exact mix depends on the business. I don't start with a fixed platform package. I start with the marketing opportunity. Then I decide where the content should go.

04

I turn the thinking into campaigns + content

This is the part where the strategy becomes something tangible. I'll create your actual content, which may include:

  • Instagram carousels
  • graphics
  • captions
  • hooks
  • CTAs
  • Threads
  • Pinterest Pins
  • Pin titles + descriptions
  • short-form content concepts
  • recurring series
  • campaign content

The exact mix is personalized to your business. You get 10 finished pieces — not 10 variations of the same generic post.

05

The pieces connect

I don't want you to receive 10 unrelated posts. I'll look for natural connections between the ideas. For example:

A customer question might become: Carousel → Thread → Pinterest Pin → future blog topic

A strong product idea might become: Campaign → recurring series → supporting content

A seasonal opportunity might become: Campaign angle → product content → discovery content → website destination

The goal isn't to create more content for the sake of creating more content. It's to build a small connected content world around what you sell.
