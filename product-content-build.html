A peek at how a Product Content Build actually comes together.

You don't need more content ideas.

You need someone to make sense of what you already have. Maybe your folder looks something like this:

📸 47 product photos 🎥 random videos from your phone 📝 notes you meant to turn into posts 💬 customer questions ⭐ reviews 🛍️ product descriptions 📱 old posts 💡 half-finished ideas

There's plenty there. But when Monday comes around: "What should I post?" So you make something. Then another thing. Then something promotional because you haven't posted in a while. And eventually your content starts feeling like a collection of unrelated posts instead of something that's actually building your brand. That's what I want to simplify.

Your product is the starting point. Not the entire content strategy.

I don't think you need to squeeze 30 posts out of one product. I think we need to look at the world around it. A candle isn't just a candle. It's:

slow evenings home self-care gifting rituals atmosphere hosting friends seasonal moments the person who buys it the life they want it to fit into

Those aren't random content ideas. They're conversations your brand can have. And those conversations can become content.

Here's how Simplify works.

01

Content Direction

I'll review your:

  • products
  • website
  • existing content
  • audience
  • current marketing
  • business priorities

Then I'll identify:

  • key content conversations
  • useful angles
  • recommended channels
  • formats
  • recurring/series opportunities
  • ways the content can connect

You don't need to arrive with a content strategy.

02

Channel Recommendation

You don't need to be everywhere. I'll recommend the channel mix that makes the most sense for your business, based on your business stage and founder personality. That could include:

Instagram

for visual storytelling, product education + brand connection

Threads

for founder-led ideas, opinions, stories + conversations

Pinterest

for search/discovery + evergreen product content

Your 10 pieces will be distributed across the channels that make the most sense for this build.

03

I build the content direction

Before I start creating, I map out what we're actually going to talk about — content themes, conversation angles, recurring topics, formats, recommended channels and series opportunities. If I spot an idea that could become a repeatable series, I'll build that into the direction too.

04

Then I actually make the content

You don't get a list of ideas and a "good luck!" — you get the actual content: carousels, graphics, captions, hooks and CTAs for Instagram; complete Threads and conversation starters; finished Pins, Pin copy, titles and descriptions for Pinterest. The exact mix is based on your business, not a one-size-fits-all package.

05

Connected Content

The pieces won't be created as 10 unrelated posts. I'll look for natural connections between them.

  • One strong idea may become multiple formats
  • A product conversation may become a recurring series
  • A customer question may become a carousel, Thread and Pin

The goal is to give your content somewhere to go.
