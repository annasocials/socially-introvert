Privacy + cookies.

What I collect, why, and how to change your mind.

Last updated October 2, 2026

Who I am

Socially Introverts is run by Anna. If you have any question about this page or your data, email sociallyintroverts@outlook.com.

What I collect

Website analytics, only if you accept. I use Google Analytics to see which pages are helping people and which are not. It records things like the pages you view, how you arrived, your device and browser type, and your approximate location, taken from your IP address.

Messages you send me. If you email me or use the inquiry form, I receive the details you type, such as your name, email address and message. I use them to reply and to deliver what you asked for. The contact and referral forms open a pre-filled email in your own email app, so nothing is sent until you press send there.

Cookies

Analytics cookies are off until you click Accept on the cookie note. If you click Decline, or don’t answer, Google Analytics does not load at all.

Who else handles your data

Google processes the analytics data on my behalf. I’m based in Singapore and work with people worldwide, so your data may be processed outside the country where you live. My pages also link to other sites, such as Pinterest, Instagram and Threads. Once you click through, their own privacy policies apply.

How long I keep it

Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in my Google Analytics account. Emails are kept for as long as I need them to deal with your request or project.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, Singapore or California), you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data I hold about you, and you can withdraw your cookie consent at any time using the button above. Email me and I’ll get back to you.

Changes

If I change this page, I’ll update the date at the top.
